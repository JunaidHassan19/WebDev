Sub & Sup Tags

The <sub> and <sup> elements are used to define subscript and superscript text, respectively.

Subscript (<sub>)

Subscript text is displayed slightly below the normal line of text. It is often used in chemical formulas, mathematical expressions, and footnotes. For example:

H2O is the chemical formula for water.

Superscript (<sup>)

Superscript text is displayed slightly above the normal line of text. It is commonly used for exponents in mathematical expressions, ordinal indicators, and footnotes. For example:

E = mc2 is Einstein's famous equation.
